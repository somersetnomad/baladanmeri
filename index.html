The method

Jean-Baptiste Morin was a French mathematician and physician who spent thirty years on his astrology in the seventeenth century. His system is strict. It does not allow me to say something will happen unless your birth chart already promises it.

And it asks four separate techniques to agree before I name a date. I only give a date where at least three of them meet. Where they do not, I say so.

Few astrologers work this way, because it is demanding. I chose it because it is honest. It shows its reasons.

How I hold the dates

I give windows, not appointments. A forecast describes pressures and openings. What you do with them is yours.

I also want to be plain about something. Astrology is not a proven science, and no honest astrologer can promise that events will arrive on time. What I can promise is care: the method applied properly, your chart studied alone, and my reasons laid out so you can weigh them yourself. Many people find that this gives them a clearer way of seeing their year. That is what I offer.

The report is not medical, legal or financial advice, and it will never tell you something frightening as fact.

Questions people ask

Why is it free?+

Because I want to hear honestly how this work lands with people before I offer it more widely. Your testimonial is the exchange. There is nothing to buy, now or later.

Does my testimonial have to be positive?+

No. Critical words help me more than kind ones. You choose whether I may share them, and how: with your name, your first name, anonymously, or not at all.

How long does it take?+

About five days from when I receive your form. If your birth time needs working out first, I will tell you.

Can I ask about one thing only?+

Yes. The form asks for your main question, and I give it the most room.

What if a date passes and nothing happens?+

Then I would like to hear about it. Sometimes a matter comes in stages, or later than the main window. The report says which dates are strong and which are tentative.

Do you use the outer planets?+

Morin worked before Uranus, Neptune and Pluto were discovered, so his method does not depend on them. I mention them where they add something.

Is my information private?+

Yes. Your details are used only to prepare your forecast. Your testimonial is shared only in the way you choose.
